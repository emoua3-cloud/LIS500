Tech heroes / Meet Ethan's Tech Hero

Afua Bruce

Engineering with communities in mind.

Technology should serve people

Afua Bruce is an engineer, author, and public interest technologist whose work connects technology, policy, and society. She has held senior science and technology positions at DataKind, the White House, the FBI, and IBM. She earned a computer engineering degree from Purdue University and an MBA from the University of Michigan.

Bruce advocates for equity through technology: her approach connects technical decisions to the people and communities affected by them. With her experience in U.S. government and nonprofit; Bruce approaches relevant questions about who benefits from public systems and whose needs get overlooked. What stands out to us is her focus on people. For someone interested in IT and cybersecurity, her work is a reminder that a successful system should be helping the people.

Tech heroes / Meet Casey's Tech Hero

Joy Buolamwini

Researcher for Fair Technology

Technology should serve people

Joy Buolamwini is a computer scientist and researcher who focuses on making technology fair for everyone. Joy studied at Georgia Tech and MIT and later founded the Algorithmic Justice League, an organization that works to bring attention to unfairness in AI.

Joy noticed that facial recognition software had trouble recognizing her face because of her darker skin complexion. This ignited her interest and advocation to research these problems within AI. Often displayed in her work, she highlights that even technology could embody bias.

What stands out to us about Joy was her dedication to take a real world experience and find a resolution to it. Her experience with facial recognition software shows us that even as technology continues advancing, it doesn't correlate to being fair and equitable.
