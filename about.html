About Us

Two UW–Madison students exploring technology, identity, and equity.

Ethan Moua

Information Science

Hi, I’m Ethan. I’m an Information Science student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison with an interest in IT and cybersecurity. I want to understand how technology works and how it affects the people who use it.

In cybersecurity, protecting people means paying attention to their needs. A system can be technically strong but still difficult for someone to access or understand. That makes questions about fairness relevant to the work I want to do.

Casey Morgan Schelling

Consumer Behavior & Marketplace Studies

Hi, I’m Casey Morgan Schelling. I’m a junior at the University of Wisconsin–Madison majoring in Consumer Behavior & Marketplace Studies with a certificate in Digital Studies. I took LIS500 because I wanted to learn more about how technology affects people and our society. Technology is such a big part of everyday life, so I find it interesting to explore both its benefits and the problems that come with it.

My favorite area of technology is artificial intelligence because of how quickly it has grown and the many different ways people use it. I don’t have one specific tech hero, but I’m interested in people who create new technology while also thinking about how it impacts society.

Why this site exists

This LIS500 project explores the relationship between technology, identity, and equity. Our goal is to make unfamiliar concepts easier to understand and encourage people to think about the assumptions they bring into everyday decisions.
